Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

MILE (Make It Look Easy) is a multisport training platform being developed and operated by an Australian sole trader under the product name MILE Training ("MILE", "we", "us" or "our"). This policy explains how personal information is handled when you use MILE, including during beta testing.

Privacy contact: alexx_vann@hotmail.com
For privacy enquiries, access, correction, account deletion or data removal requests, contact us at this address.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, MILE may collect:

  • Account and profile information: email address, account identifiers, display name, username, profile photo, cover photo, biography, selected sports and privacy preferences.
  • Training information: activities, workout titles, dates, distances, durations, pace, heart rate, training plans, personal bests, goals and other information you enter or import.
  • Health and wellness information: metrics such as sleep, resting heart rate, HRV, recovery and self-reported fatigue where you choose to provide or connect them. These may be sensitive personal information.
  • Social information: posts, captions, comments, likes, follow requests, messages and notification preferences.
  • Technical information: information reasonably necessary for login, security, troubleshooting and operation of the service, which may include browser, device and network information processed by our service providers.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide and maintain accounts, generate and display training plans and performance insights, sync workouts, support recovery-related features, enable user-selected social interactions, respond to support requests, prevent misuse, troubleshoot problems and improve MILE. MILE is a training tool, not a medical diagnosis or treatment service.

3. Garmin and other connected fitness services

MILE is seeking official Garmin Connect Developer Program access. Official Garmin account connection is not yet generally available. If enabled in the future, you will be asked to authorise access through Garmin's approved connection process. We will request only the data categories needed for the features offered, subject to the permissions and access Garmin provides.

Connected activity and, where authorised, health information may be used to populate your personal dashboard, training history, performance insights and recovery features. Connecting a fitness service will be optional. You may revoke a connected service's access through the controls made available by that service and may contact us to request removal of information previously imported into MILE.

We will not sell Garmin-derived personal data. We will not intentionally publish your private health metrics or detailed location routes to other users without an appropriate sharing choice and permission. Workout-sharing features may make selected summaries visible according to your settings. Additional connected services, if introduced, will be described in the app or this policy.

4. Profiles, activity sharing and messaging

MILE includes social features. Information you choose to make public, including profile details, workout summaries, photos and comments, may be seen by other users. Private profiles and follower approvals are intended to restrict access to profile content; visibility may also depend on individual activity-sharing settings. Messages are intended for their participants and authorised service operations.

Please avoid including sensitive information in public posts. We may need to access or remove content where reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, comply with law or protect the service.

5. Service providers and disclosures

MILE currently uses Supabase for application backend functions such as authentication, database services and storage, and GitHub Pages for website hosting. These providers may process data as necessary to provide their services. Hosting, infrastructure or other providers may change as MILE develops; we will update this policy for material changes.

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information to service providers acting on our behalf, where you direct or authorise us to share it, or where reasonably required by applicable law. Service providers may process or store information outside Australia, depending on their infrastructure and settings.

6. Security

We use access controls and other reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the service's stage of development. No online system is completely secure. You should use a strong password and contact us if you believe your account has been compromised.

7. Retention and deletion

We generally retain account and training information while your account is active or as needed to operate the service. You can request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information or account by emailing alexx_vann@hotmail.com. We will assess and action requests within a reasonable period, subject to identity verification, legal obligations, security needs and technical limitations such as backups. Some content shared with other users or retained for legal reasons may require separate handling.

Disconnecting a connected fitness account stops future authorised access but may not automatically erase information previously imported. Contact us if you also want imported data removed.

8. Your choices and rights

You may manage available profile visibility, activity-sharing and notification preferences in MILE. You may request information about the personal data we hold, ask for corrections or deletion, or raise a privacy complaint using the contact email above. If you are dissatisfied with our response, you may seek guidance from the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au), where applicable.

9. Children

MILE's current beta is intended for adults. We do not knowingly invite children to create accounts. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate authorisation, contact us.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as MILE evolves, including when official Garmin integration, subscriptions or new providers become available. The effective date above will be revised when changes are published. Material changes may also be communicated through the service where practical.
